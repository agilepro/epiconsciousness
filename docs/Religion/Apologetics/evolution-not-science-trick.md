# Evolution is not Science Trick

Creationists say:  "__Evolution is not science. Science has to be repeatable, observable and measurable. Evolution is none of these things we call the scientific method.__"

This is a common argument against evolutionary theory, and there are strong, well-established scientific responses to each point.

* **On repeatability**: Evolution is repeatable and has been observed experimentally many times. The most famous example is Richard Lenski's E. coli long-term evolution experiment, running since 1988, which has directly observed bacteria evolving new metabolic capabilities over tens of thousands of generations. Antibiotic resistance evolving in bacteria, pesticide resistance in insects, and the evolution of nylon-digesting bacteria are all documented, repeatable phenomena. Creationists sometimes concede this as "microevolution" but the distinction between micro and macro is not one biology recognizes as a scientific boundary — it's the same process over longer timescales.

* **On observability**: Evolution is directly observable. We have observed speciation — new species forming — in real time, in both the lab and the wild. Hawaiian silversword plants, Italian wall lizards transplanted to a new island, and numerous insect and plant species have been observed speciating within decades. The fossil record also provides a sequential, observable record of change over time. Observation doesn't require a human watching every step, just as we observe the history of a star through light without watching it form. Also see [Evolution Never Observed Trick](./observing-evolution-trick.md).

* **On measurability**: Evolution is extremely measurable. Allele frequencies in populations are precisely quantified. Phylogenetic trees are built from measurable DNA differences. The molecular clock uses mutation rates to calculate divergence times with testable precision. Population genetics is a deeply mathematical field.

* **On the broader framing**: The argument conflates "repeatable in a lab in real time" with what science actually requires. Historical sciences — geology, astronomy, forensics, cosmology — all reconstruct past events through evidence, models, and testable predictions without directly "rerunning" them. We don't re-ignite supernovae to confirm they happened. The same logical standards apply to evolution. A geologist can't repeat the formation of the Grand Canyon but can test predictions about the process rigorously.

It's also worth noting that evolution is accepted not just by biologists but by geneticists, virologists, anthropologists, and medical researchers — fields where evolutionary predictions have practical consequences. 

The best response to the claim is usually to ask: **which specific prediction of evolutionary theory has been tested and failed**, because that's the actual standard of science — and evolution has an extraordinary track record of making and passing those tests.

## What is Takes to be Scientific

> "A theory is a good theory if it satisfies two requirements. It must accurately describe a large class of observations on the basis of a model that contains only a few arbitrary elements and it must make definite predictions about the results of future observations." - Stephen Hawking, A Brief History of Time.

Evolution satisfies both of these requirements and then some. It explains a huge body of data and answers a substantial number of significant questions: "Why do vestigials and atavisms exist?" For instance. It boldly predicted countless results of future observations, such as the location of Tiktaalik roseae and the discovery of hundreds of whale ancestor intermediates.

Claiming that evolution is unscientific is one of the most  desperate tactics evolution deniers attempt to use. They weaponize a middle school level understanding of what science is and how it works to implant the false perception that science proves things beyond any and all doubts. It's then extrapoled that because evolution has not been "proven" beyond all doubts, it is not "science."

Evolution is not a theory in crisis. It is not teetering on the verge of collapse. It has not failed as a scientific explanation. There is evidence for evolution, gobs and gobs of it. It is not just speculation or a faith choice or an assumption or a religion. It is a productive framework for lots of biological research, and it has amazing explanatory power. There is no conspiracy to hide the truth about the failure of evolution. There has really been no failure of evolution as a scientific theory. It works, and it works well.

Evolution itself is not flawed or without evidence. Please don't be duped into thinking that somehow evolution itself is a failure. Please don't idolize your own ability to reason.
